        Questions people ask

        Is anything uploaded?

        No. Both tools run inside your browser and process files on your device. Open the Network tab while you work to check.

        Do I need internet?

        Not after you download the files. Both tools work with the internet turned off.

        Which browsers and computers work?

        Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome on a desktop computer (Windows, macOS or Linux). Screen capture is not designed for phones.

        Which formats can I save?

        The converter saves MP4, MOV or MKV. The recorder saves MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM, plus PNG screenshots.

        What do I get after buying?

        An instant download with both tools as HTML files, the lib folder, a PDF user manual, a quick start guide and license notices.

        Can I use it for commercial videos?

        Yes. The videos you make belong to you. Reselling or sharing the tools themselves is not allowed.

        Can I add text, logos and images to my video?

        Yes. Place images and styled text on the preview, animate the text, and choose on the timeline when each one shows. They are included in the converted video when you join your clips into one video.

        Are there file size limits?

        The tools use your browser memory, so files over about 1 GB should be cut into parts first. The converter can split long videos automatically.
