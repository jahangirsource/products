        Questions readers ask

        What is the book about?

        It explores thirteen values for success and inner peace, such as honesty, effort, patience, gratitude and consultation. Each chapter pairs a true story with a principle from the Quran and practical steps.

        Who is it for?

        Professionals, students and business owners who want success that does not cost them their peace of mind.

        Do I need to be Muslim to read it?

        The book is written from an Islamic perspective and draws on Quranic values. Readers of any background who value honesty, patience and fairness may also find the stories and ideas useful.

        Which format is it, and how do I read it?

        It is an ebook that you read in the store’s own reading app, such as Apple Books, on a phone, tablet or computer.

        Can I read a sample first?

        On Apple Books, use the Get Sample button on the book page to preview the opening pages.

        Does it promise wealth?

        No. It shares values and real stories. It does not promise a particular result.
